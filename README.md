## Overview

This project takes one product, the **HydroForce HF-250 PRO** industrial pressure washer, from a market case through process design to a complete factory layout. It was delivered in three submissions and is built around a single thread:

> Market need > Product > Process > Facility

## Project Objectives

1. Establish the business case (market survey, pricing, breakeven and five-year financials).
2. Design the product for manufacture (make-or-buy decision, drawings, exploded view).
3. Analyse the production process (routings, process charts, master flow patterns).
4. Plan the facility (activity relationships, space planning, MODAPTS time standards, line balancing).
5. Produce a final drawn layout with efficient flow, minimal backtracking and safe operations.

## The Product

| Feature | Specification |
|---|---|
| Pressure | 250 bar (3,626 PSI), adjustable 50 to 250 bar |
| Flow rate | 15 L/min rated, 21 L/min maximum |
| Power | 7.5 kW electric, 400 V three-phase |
| Pump | Triplex ceramic plunger pump, stainless-steel manifold |
| Frame | Powder-coated steel chassis, heavy-duty caster wheels |
| Warranty | 24 months on pump and motor |
| Compliance | SANS/IEC |

**Components:** 14 in total, with 10 made in-house and 4 bought in (AC motor, high-pressure pump, high-pressure hose, caster wheels).

## Project Structure

### Part 1: Business Case & Product Design (Jan to Mar 2026)
- Company overview, product description and management structure
- Market survey and questionnaire (150 respondents)
- Competitive landscape and pricing strategy
- Capital requirements, breakeven analysis, ROI and five-year income statement
- Bill of materials and make-or-buy decision
- Detailed drawings (D1 to D14) and exploded view (SolidWorks)

### Part 2: Process Analysis & Master Flow (Mar to Apr 2026)
- Production routings: 57 operations across the 10 made parts
- Assembly chart (sub-assemblies SA-1, SA-2, SA-4, SA-6; operations A1 to A8)
- Operation process charts and flow process charts
- Flow diagrams for each part
- Two master flow patterns, evaluated on 10 weighted criteria
- Facility flow analysis report (Pattern A vs Pattern B)

### Part 3: Facility, Operations & Layout (Apr to Sep 2026)
- Activity relationship chart, worksheet and diagram
- Space planning for 12 areas (receiving, storage, maintenance, tool room, locker room, toilets, production, offices, cafeteria, medical room, warehouse, shipping)
- MODAPTS time studies and line balancing for each part
- Production line layouts and area layouts (AutoCAD)
- Final combined facility layout

## Key Methods

- **Market and financial analysis:** survey, breakeven, ROI and income statement
- **Process analysis:** routings, operation and flow process charts
- **Flow evaluation:** weighted-criteria comparison of master flow patterns
- **Systematic layout planning:** activity relationship analysis and space planning
- **Work measurement:** MODAPTS (0.00215 min per MOD) with a 15% allowance

## Opening the Files

- `.drawio` files open in [draw.io](https://app.diagrams.net) or the desktop app.
- `.dwg` and `.dwf` files need AutoCAD or a compatible viewer.
- `.xlsx`, `.docx` and `.pptx` files open in Microsoft Office or a compatible suite.
